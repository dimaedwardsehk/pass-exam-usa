FAQ

What is fiduciary duty in real estate?

Fiduciary duty in real estate refers to the obligation of real estate professionals to act in the best interest of their clients, maintaining confidentiality and avoiding conflicts of interest.

How does fiduciary duty apply to different agency relationships?

Fiduciary duty applies differently to various agency relationships, including single agency, dual agency, and transactional brokerage. Each type of relationship has its own set of fiduciary duties and responsibilities.
