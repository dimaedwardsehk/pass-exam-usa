Introduction to Tanker Endorsement

The tanker endorsement is a specialized license that allows drivers to operate tanker vehicles, which are used to transport liquids, gases, and other hazardous materials. To obtain this endorsement, you need to pass a written test and a skills test, which cover various topics, including safety procedures, vehicle inspection, and emergency response.

The written test is a critical component of the tanker endorsement process, and it's essential to prepare well for it. The test covers a range of topics, including liquid surge, cargo handling, and safety protocols, and it's designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of these critical areas.
