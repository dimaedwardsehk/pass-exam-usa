Introduction to CDL Passenger Endorsement

The CDL passenger endorsement is required for drivers who want to operate vehicles designed to transport 16 or more passengers, including the driver. This endorsement requires a separate knowledge test and a skills test.

To prepare for the knowledge test, you should review the CDL manual and practice with sample questions. It's also essential to understand the safety topics, such as road signs, air brakes, and safe driving rules.
