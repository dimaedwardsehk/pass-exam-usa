Introduction to CDL Endorsements

A CDL endorsement is a special designation on your commercial driver's license that indicates you have passed a specific test or have completed additional training. The passenger endorsement is one of the most common endorsements, and it requires a separate test. To pass the test, you need to demonstrate your knowledge of safety procedures, passenger management, and vehicle operation.

The test is divided into several sections, each covering a different aspect of passenger transportation. You will be tested on your knowledge of road signs, safety procedures, and vehicle operation. You will also be required to demonstrate your ability to manage passengers and handle emergency situations.
