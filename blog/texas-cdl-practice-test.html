Introduction to CDL

A commercial driver's license (CDL) is required to operate a commercial vehicle in the United States. The CDL test is designed to ensure that drivers have the knowledge and skills necessary to operate a commercial vehicle safely.

To prepare for the CDL test, you should start by reviewing the core rules and regulations. This includes understanding the different types of CDLs, such as Class A, Class B, and Class C, as well as the various endorsements, such as hazardous materials and tanker endorsements.

Test Day

On test day, it's essential to be well-prepared and confident. Make sure you arrive early and have all the necessary documents, such as your driver's license and proof of residency.

During the test, read each question carefully and take your time to answer. If you're unsure of an answer, try to eliminate any obviously incorrect options and make an educated guess.
