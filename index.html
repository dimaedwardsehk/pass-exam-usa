A buyer signs a purchase agreement but the seller has not yet accepted. At this point, the buyer is known as the:
A Vendee
B Grantee
C Offeror
D Optionee
Correct!
The buyer who signs and submits an offer is the offeror. They become the vendee only after the seller accepts. The grantee receives the deed at closing, and an optionee holds an option to purchase.

This is how our AI explains every answer in the app.
Not quite.
The correct answer is C) Offeror. The buyer who signs and submits an offer is the offeror. They become the vendee only after the seller accepts.
